# Instagram-Performance-Analytics-for-Sugar-Cosmetics
Analysis of 50,000 Sugar Cosmetics Instagram posts using Tableau to track reach, engagement, hashtags, and posting patterns, with data-backed content strategy recommendations.
Sugar Cosmetics Instagram Performance Analytics

GUVI x HCL Capstone Project

This project analyzes 50,000 Instagram posts from Sugar Cosmetics to understand what drives reach and engagement. The dataset covers post date, post type, campaign type, hashtags, audience emotion, engagement source, impressions, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, profile visits, and follows. I built a Tableau Public dashboard and turned the findings into a content strategy.

Tools: Tableau Public, Excel, PowerPoint

What I analyzed (10 tasks):

Overall account health (total posts, reach, comments)
Posts per month
Top 10 posts by likes
Top posts by engagement rate
Reels vs Post vs Story performance
Engagement by weekday
Engagement funnel (reach to likes, comments, shares, saves)
Top 5 hashtags by reach
One interactive dashboard combining all views
Key takeaways and posting strategy

Key findings:

50,000 posts generated about 987M total reach and 18.46M comments
Posting volume is uneven, with the peak in May and a sharp dip in February
Reels, Posts and Stories perform almost the same, so format is not the main lever
Tuesday is the best day, followed by Monday and Wednesday
Comments are only about 1.9% of reach, showing a large drop-off from reach to action
Top hashtags have very similar reach, and the highest-engagement posts differ from the most-liked ones
